{%- comment -%} Click-to-load video embed. Shows a thumbnail + play button; the third-party player (and its ads/JS) loads only when the reader clicks. Without JS, the button is replaced by a plain link to the source page. Params: id unique slug for this embed on the page title video title (used for the button label and iframe title) embed player embed URL (no autoplay param; one is added on click) thumb thumbnail image URL href source article URL (fallback link) source outlet name, e.g. "WFRV Local 5" caption optional caption text (plain text) Usage: {% include video-facade.html id="wfrv-snm" title="..." embed="..." thumb="..." href="..." source="WFRV Local 5" caption="..." %} {%- endcomment -%}
