# Urogynecological conditions

I specialize in diagnosing and treating pelvic floor disorders in women. Here are the conditions I see most often.

## [Pelvic organ prolapse](/conditions/pelvic-organ-prolapse)

Pelvic organ prolapse is when the bladder, uterus, top of the vagina, or rectum drops from its normal position and presses into the vagina, sometimes past the opening. It happens when the muscles and connective tissue that support these organs stretch or weaken. Most women notice a bulge or a feeling of pressure.

## [Urinary incontinence](/conditions/urinary-incontinence)

Involuntary leakage of urine. The main types are:
- [Stress incontinence](/conditions/stress-urinary-incontinence): leakage with coughing, sneezing, laughing, or exercise
- [Urge incontinence](/conditions/urge-urinary-incontinence): a sudden, intense urge to urinate followed by leakage
- [Mixed incontinence](/conditions/mixed-urinary-incontinence): a combination of both

## [Overactive bladder (OAB)](/conditions/overactive-bladder)

Sudden urges to urinate that are hard to control, with or without leaking. Most people with OAB also urinate often during the day and wake up at night to go.

## [Recurrent urinary tract infections (UTIs)](/conditions/recurrent-urinary-tract-infections)

Recurrent UTI means at least two bladder infections in 6 months, each confirmed with a urine culture. I look for what is driving the infections and build a prevention plan with you.

## Interstitial cystitis / painful bladder syndrome

Now often called bladder pain syndrome. It causes pain, pressure, or discomfort in the bladder, often worse as the bladder fills, and usually comes with urinary frequency and urgency. The diagnosis requires symptoms for at least 3 months with no other cause, such as infection, found.

## [Fecal incontinence](/conditions/fecal-incontinence)

The inability to control bowel movements, leading to unexpected leakage of stool. You may also hear it called accidental bowel leakage.

## [Vaginal fistulas](/conditions/vaginal-fistula)

Abnormal connections between the vagina and other pelvic organs (like the bladder or rectum) that can cause leakage of urine or stool into the vagina.

## Pelvic pain

Chronic pelvic pain has many possible causes. In my patients it often overlaps with other pelvic floor problems, such as tight or painful pelvic floor muscles, bladder pain, or pain with intercourse.

## [Genitourinary syndrome of menopause (GSM)](/conditions/genitourinary-syndrome-of-menopause)

Previously called vaginal atrophy. GSM covers the vaginal, vulvar, and urinary changes caused by falling estrogen during and after menopause: vaginal dryness, irritation, painful intercourse, urinary urgency and frequency, burning with urination, and recurrent urinary tract infections.
